## 1. General Information

### 1.1 Purpose
This sample describes fictional internal workplace procedures. It helps demonstrate how PA-LAW distinguishes company-specific questions from statutory labor questions.

### 1.2 Scope
The sample covers fictional regular, probationary, project-based, and fixed-term employees. Actual coverage in a real organization must be determined from applicable law and employment agreements.

### 1.3 Policy interpretation
If a provision conflicts with applicable law or a valid government issuance, applicable law takes precedence. This handbook must not be used to reduce a statutory employee entitlement.

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance

### 2.1 Sample work schedule
For demonstration only, the fictional office schedule is Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with a one-hour meal break. Employees record attendance using the approved attendance system. This sample schedule is not a determination of the legally applicable hours for any real employee.

### 2.2 Attendance correction
An employee who forgets to time in or time out should notify their supervisor and submit an attendance correction request to HR. The request should include the date, affected time, and explanation.

### 2.3 Absence notification
Employees should notify their immediate supervisor as soon as reasonably possible if they will be late or absent. HR records and reviews attendance matters under approved procedures and applicable law.

**Responsible department:** Human Resources.

## 3. Leave and Time-Off Policies

> The leave categories and procedures below are fictional examples. They do not establish statutory leave eligibility or replace Philippine law.

### 3.1 Annual leave request
For this demonstration, employees submit leave requests through the HR portal. The fictional target is to submit requests at least five working days before the intended leave, when practicable. The supervisor reviews scheduling, and HR checks the leave record and applicable policy. The five-day target is not a statutory rule.

### 3.2 Sick leave notification
An employee unable to work due to illness should notify their supervisor and HR as soon as practicable. HR may request appropriate supporting documents when permitted by law and company policy.

### 3.3 Emergency leave
An employee facing an urgent personal or family situation should notify their supervisor as soon as practicable and coordinate with HR about the applicable leave category and documentation. Eligibility, pay treatment, and required documents depend on applicable law, the employee's circumstances, and the actual approved company policy. This sample does not promise a specific number of emergency-leave days.

### 3.4 Statutory leave questions
Questions about maternity leave, paternity leave, solo parent leave, service incentive leave, leave for victims of violence against women and their children, and other statutory leaves must be checked against current Philippine law and official government guidance. HR should verify eligibility and requirements using authoritative sources. This fictional handbook does not independently establish statutory entitlements.

**Responsible department:** Human Resources.

## 4. Compensation and Payroll

### 4.1 Sample payroll schedule
For demonstration only, the fictional company targets payroll release on the 15th and last calendar day of each month. If a target date falls on a non-working day or a banking disruption occurs, Payroll will issue an advisory. This is a fictional company schedule, not a statement of the statutory wage-payment deadline.

### 4.2 Payslip questions
Employees who notice a possible payslip error should contact Finance and Payroll. Provide the payroll period and a description of the suspected discrepancy. Share sensitive details only through approved secure channels. Do not post bank account numbers, passwords, or identity documents in the chatbot.

### 4.3 Salary discrepancy or unpaid salary
A report that salary was not received, was underpaid, or contains an unexplained deduction is treated as a **complaint** for PA-LAW routing. Route it to Finance and Payroll for record verification and human review. Only authorized staff may issue the official response.

**Responsible department:** Finance and Payroll.

## 5. Benefits and 13th-Month Pay

### 5.1 General questions
Questions about eligibility, computation, coverage, and payment deadlines for 13th-month pay must be answered using the applicable DOLE handbook and current official issuances. The company handbook may describe internal payroll processing but must not replace or contradict statutory requirements.

### 5.2 Individual payment status
For a question about when an employee's own 13th-month pay will be credited, PA-LAW may provide general DOLE information from its knowledge base. Authorized Finance staff must verify the employee's actual payroll status.

### 5.3 Missing or incorrect payment
A report that 13th-month pay was not received or was allegedly computed incorrectly is a **complaint**. Route it to Finance and Payroll for human review. Do not decide that the employer complied with or violated the law based only on a short chat message.

**Responsible departments:** Finance and Payroll; Human Resources for policy coordination.

## 6. Overtime and Holiday Work

### 6.1 Overtime request
For this fictional organization, employees should obtain supervisor authorization before planned overtime whenever practicable. The request should state the date, expected hours, business reason, and approving supervisor. Emergency or unplanned work should be documented and reported as soon as practicable.

### 6.2 Overtime pay questions
Questions about overtime rates, rest-day work, special days, regular holidays, and night shift differential must be answered using current applicable Philippine labor rules and the DOLE handbook. If necessary inputs or evidence are missing, PA-LAW should ask for clarification or refer the matter to HR/Payroll.

### 6.3 Disputed overtime payment
A complaint that approved or reported overtime was not paid should be routed to Finance and Payroll for human review.

**Responsible departments:** Immediate Supervisor; Finance and Payroll; Human Resources.

## 7. Reimbursements and Cash Advances

### 7.1 Business expense reimbursement
For this demonstration, employees should submit reimbursement requests within ten working days after the expense, where practicable. This is a fictional administrative target.

Sample supporting information:
- Business purpose
- Expense date
- Amount and currency
- Department or project
- Receipt or acceptable supporting document
- Supervisor approval, if required

### 7.2 Reimbursement processing
The employee submits the request; the supervisor verifies the business purpose; Finance checks documents and arithmetic; Finance then approves, returns, or rejects the request with an explanation. Approved reimbursements are included in the next applicable payment run.

### 7.3 Missing documents
If a receipt or required information is missing, Finance may place the request on hold and ask for the missing information. PA-LAW must not invent a supplier, receipt, amount, or approval.

### 7.4 Cash advances
Cash advances require prior authorization under the fictional internal approval process. Employees submit a liquidation report and supporting documents within the period stated in the approved cash-advance form.

**Responsible department:** Finance.

## 8. Employee Conduct and Workplace Complaints

### 8.1 Respectful workplace
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and visitors respectfully. Discrimination, harassment, threats, retaliation, and serious misconduct should be reported through an appropriate company channel.

### 8.2 Filing a complaint
An employee may report a concern to HR or an authorized reporting officer. Where possible, include what happened, approximate date and location, people or roles involved, available supporting information, and whether immediate assistance is needed. Do not disclose sensitive details through an unapproved channel.

### 8.3 Human review requirement
All complaints must be referred to an authorized human reviewer. PA-LAW may classify and route a complaint, but it must not decide whether an allegation is true, determine disciplinary action, promise an outcome, or send an official finding without human approval.

### 8.4 Confidentiality
Complaint information should be restricted to people who need it to review and address the matter. Actual procedures must be reviewed against applicable law and organizational requirements.

**Responsible department:** Human Resources.

## 9. Information Technology and Data Security

### 9.1 Account and password issues
Employees who cannot access a company account should contact the IT Helpdesk. Passwords and one-time verification codes must never be shared with PA-LAW or another person.

### 9.2 Equipment and software
Requests for laptops, software, network access, and equipment repair should be submitted through the IT service request process.

### 9.3 Security incident
Report suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized access, or lost company equipment to IT immediately through the approved incident channel. Do not upload confidential source code, credentials, customer records, or sensitive employee data to the chatbot.

**Responsible department:** Information Technology.

## 10. Health, Safety, and Emergency Procedures

### 10.1 Immediate danger
If a person faces immediate danger, injury, fire, or a medical emergency, contact appropriate emergency responders and follow the organization's emergency procedures. Do not wait for a chatbot response.

### 10.2 Workplace safety concern
Report unsafe equipment, blocked exits, electrical hazards, or other workplace hazards to Administration or the designated safety officer.

**Responsible departments:** Administration; designated Safety Officer.

## 11. Department Responsibilities and Routing

| Department | Typical topics | Example message |
|---|---|---|
| Human Resources | Leave procedures, attendance, employment records, workplace complaints, HR policies | How do I request emergency leave? |
| Finance and Payroll | Salary, payslips, payroll discrepancies, reimbursements, cash advances, payment status | My salary was not credited. |
| Information Technology | Passwords, accounts, software, network, computers, cybersecurity | I cannot access my company email. |
| Administration | Office facilities, supplies, building maintenance, workplace equipment | The office air conditioner is broken. |
| Operations | Work assignments, service delivery, project coordination, operational procedures | Who approves this project handover? |
| Manual Routing | Unclear, mixed, sensitive, or unsupported concerns | I am not sure which department handles this. |

### 11.1 PA-LAW routing rules
- A complaint is routed to the most appropriate department and marked for human review.
- An inquiry is routed to the appropriate department and may receive a handbook-grounded response if sufficient evidence is retrieved.
- If the department is ambiguous, request clarification or route for manual review.
- If a question requires employee-specific records, an authorized department must verify those records.
- Do not present a statutory rule as an internal company procedure unless the company source supports it.

## 12. Policy Questions, Complaints, and Review Process

### 12.1 Inquiry workflow
1. Employee submits a policy question.
2. PA-LAW classifies it as an inquiry.
3. Python validates the classification and department.
4. RAG retrieves relevant passages from the DOLE handbook and/or this company handbook.
5. The system checks whether the evidence supports an answer.
6. The LLM drafts an answer using retrieved evidence.
7. PA-LAW displays the answer and source citations.
8. If evidence is insufficient, PA-LAW explains the limitation and refers the question to the appropriate department.

### 12.2 Complaint workflow
1. Employee submits a complaint.
2. PA-LAW classifies it as a complaint.
3. Python validates the classification and department.
4. The complaint is routed to the appropriate department.
5. The workflow state becomes `WAITING_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW`.
6. An authorized staff member assesses the concern.
7. The staff member prepares and approves the official response.
8. The employee is informed through an approved company channel.

### 12.3 Source priority
- Use the DOLE handbook and current official government sources for statutory labor questions.
- Use this company handbook for the fictional organization's internal procedures.
- Use both when a question involves statutory rights and internal administration.
- If sources conflict, flag the conflict for HR/legal review.
- If no source supports an answer, state that the available knowledge base does not establish it.

### 12.4 Citations
A RAG-generated response should identify the document title, section or subsection, original PDF page number where applicable, and source type. Do not fabricate citations or page numbers.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. How do I request emergency leave?
Notify your supervisor as soon as practicable and coordinate with HR about the applicable leave category and documentation. Eligibility and pay treatment depend on applicable law and the actual approved company policy.  
**Routing:** Human Resources — **Type:** Inquiry

### Q2. My salary was not credited. What should I do?
Report the issue to Finance and Payroll with the affected payroll period and relevant payslip details. An authorized staff member must verify the payroll record.  
**Routing:** Finance and Payroll — **Type:** Complaint

### Q3. When will I receive my 13th-month pay?
Check statutory timing and eligibility against the DOLE handbook and current official guidance. Contact Finance and Payroll for your individual payment status.  
**Routing:** Human Resources or Finance and Payroll — **Type:** Inquiry

### Q4. How do I request reimbursement?
Submit the expense purpose, date, amount, supporting receipt, and required approval through the reimbursement process. Finance reviews the submission.  
**Routing:** Finance — **Type:** Inquiry

### Q5. I cannot access my company email. Who can help?
Contact the IT Helpdesk. Never share your password or one-time verification code.  
**Routing:** Information Technology — **Type:** Inquiry

### Q6. I want to report unfair treatment at work. What should I do?
Contact HR or an authorized reporting officer. A human reviewer must assess the concern; the chatbot does not decide the merits of a complaint.  
**Routing:** Human Resources — **Type:** Complaint

### Q7. My reimbursement was rejected. Can PA-LAW approve it?
No. PA-LAW can explain the documented process and route the question, but only authorized Finance staff can review and decide the reimbursement request.  
**Routing:** Finance — **Type:** Depends on message intent
